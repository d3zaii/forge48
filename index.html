09 / FAQ

QUESTIONS?
GOOD.

Who can participate?

Students, early builders, designers, developers and curious problem-solvers from across India. Teams can have 1–4 members.

Is it completely online?

Yes. The challenge, mentorship, submissions and demos are designed to run online.

Do I need to be an expert?

No. You need a willingness to build. Pick a problem that matches your current skills and collaborate with teammates where you can.

Are internships guaranteed?

No. Top-performing participants may be considered for remote project-based internship opportunities, subject to selection and eligibility.

What happens after registration?

You receive the event brief, challenge details and joining instructions before the opening session.
